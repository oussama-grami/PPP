Additional Insights

Understanding the Percentages:

🔋 Emission Intensity Reduction ({{ getReductionPercentage() | number : "1.1-1" }}%): This shows that solar panels have {{ getReductionPercentage() | number : "1.1-1" }}% lower emissions than {{ getSelectedCountryName() }}'s grid electricity per kWh. This percentage is constant because it compares the inherent efficiency of the two energy sources.

🎯 Project Scale Impact ({{ getProjectScalePercentage() | number : "1.1-1" }}%): This shows how much your {{ panneauxForm.get("consommation")?.value | number }} kWh project contributes to meaningful environmental change. This percentage varies based on project size.

Think of it like comparing car fuel efficiency (constant %) vs. how much gas you actually save based on how much you drive (variable %).

🔍
Why 86.4% is Always the Same

Mathematical Proof:
• Tunisia grid: 441g CO2/kWh
• Solar panels: 60g CO2/kWh
• Reduction: (441-60)/441 = 86.4%

This is like saying "electric cars are always 90% more efficient than gas cars" - the percentage is constant regardless of how many miles you drive. Your project size affects total impact, not efficiency percentage.
