# 🌍 EcoPilot

**EcoPilot** is an AI-powered platform that enables companies to **measure**, **reduce**, and **offset** their carbon emissions in a simple, actionable, and intelligent way. With advanced forecasting, real-time dashboards, and ESG assessments, EcoPilot empowers organizations to become leaders in sustainability.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1️⃣ Carbon Footprint Calculator
- Calculates your company’s emissions from electricity, transportation, logistics, waste, production, etc.
- Provides accurate ML-based results and personalized emission reduction strategies.

### 2️⃣ ESG Assessment
- Evaluate your Environmental, Social, and Governance performance.
- Generate a score, receive tailored recommendations, and track progress over time.

### 3️⃣ Emissions Forecasting
- Predict future carbon emissions using AI models trained on historical data.
- Visualize trends through interactive dashboards and store custom projections.

### 4️⃣ Offset & Compensation
- Access a verified marketplace for carbon offset projects like tree planting and renewable energy.
- Propose and fund your own ecological initiatives.

### 5️⃣ Authentication & Security
- Role-based access control, GitHub/email login, secure HTTPS data transmission.
- Strong password policies and account management with Keycloak.

### 6️⃣ Performance & Usability
- Fast, cross-platform, responsive interface.
- Real-time feedback and 99% uptime for smooth user experience.
